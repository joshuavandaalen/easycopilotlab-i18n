## 5 Things the Completed Agent Can Do

By the end of today's course, you'll have an agent that can do all 5 of the following.

### 1. Automatic FAQ Answers

For questions like "How many vacation days do I have left?", it will automatically answer **based on the uploaded documents (textbook)**, with citations.

### 2. Contact Lookup

"Who handles expense reimbursements?" → It instantly retrieves **name, phone number, and email** from an Excel file.

### 3. Benefits Guidance

"Tell me where I can use welfare points" → It answers by **referencing multiple documents simultaneously**.
It's like a new hire carrying several textbooks at once.

### 4. Forwarding Inquiries by Email

"I'd like to contact the person in charge directly" → The agent confirms the person's email address, organizes your inquiry, and **automatically forwards it as an email (envelope)**.

### 5. Auto-Saving Chat Logs

Who asked what, when — it's **automatically recorded in Excel**.
From an admin's perspective, you can see the agent's usage at a glance.
